Welcome to Senative Villa

A Tranquil Retreat for Explorers of Sri Lanka's South

Senative Villa is a modern, boutique guest property in Kadewatta, Weligama — positioned at the heart of Sri Lanka's most beautiful southern coastline. Whether you are here to surf Weligama's legendary waves, watch blue whales at Mirissa, cycle to the coconut tree hill, or simply unwind with the sound of tropical birdsong, our villa is your ideal base.

The property features two independent, fully self-contained apartments, each thoughtfully designed to give guests complete comfort and privacy. You are not in a hotel room — you are in your own home, with your own kitchen, living area, and bathroom.

Perfect for couples, solo travellers, families, and digital nomads who want more than just a bed — they want a home.

What's Included

Everything You Need for a Comfortable Stay

Every apartment is carefully equipped so guests can settle in immediately and feel completely at home.

A/C Bedrooms

Each apartment has a private air-conditioned bedroom with a comfortable king-size bed, fresh linens, pillows, and wardrobe space for a cool and restful night's sleep.

Private Washroom

A clean, modern en-suite bathroom with hot water rainfall shower, illuminated LED vanity mirror, bidet hand shower, and quality sanitary fittings — fully tiled in crisp white.

Private Living Room

Each apartment opens into a spacious and airy private living and dining room, furnished with a handcrafted wooden dining table and chairs, plus comfortable lounge seating.

Kitchen Pantry

A dedicated private pantry area with counter space, kitchen sink, and ample storage — giving you the freedom to prepare your own meals, snacks, and morning coffee.

Refrigerator

Each apartment has its own dedicated refrigerator, so you can safely store fresh groceries, leftovers, chilled beverages, and tropical fruits from the local market.

High-Speed Wi-Fi

Stay connected with fast fiber-optic broadband internet throughout the entire property. Great for remote workers, streaming, social media, or keeping in touch with home.

Washing Machine

An automatic washing machine is available on the premises. Keep your clothes fresh throughout a longer stay without the need for expensive laundry services.

Kitchen Utensils

Fully stocked with cooking pots, frying pans, plates, bowls, cups, glasses, cutlery, an electric kettle, and everything you need for everyday cooking and dining.

Free Parking

Enjoy free and secure on-site parking for your motorbike, tuk-tuk, or rental car. Easy road access with a quiet neighbourhood setting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before planning your stay at Senative Villa.
